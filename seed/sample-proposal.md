# AI Strategy & Implementation Engagement — Healing Hands Wellness Studio

## Engagement Overview

Thank you for the opportunity to support Healing Hands Wellness Studio's growth into AI-assisted client services. This proposal outlines a focused 10-week engagement to design, build, and deploy an intelligent scheduling and intake assistant that reduces administrative burden while preserving the warm, personal experience your clients expect.

All work is delivered by Ben Dudley, Principal, Agentic Arc AI — a solo consultancy specializing in practical AI for small and mid-size service businesses.

## Scope of Work

This engagement covers three interconnected workstreams:

**Workstream 1 — Discovery & Design (Weeks 1–2)**
Structured interviews with your front-desk team and lead practitioners. Review of your current scheduling tool (MindBody), intake forms, and cancellation workflows. Delivery: a one-page AI opportunity map and a prioritized feature list.

**Workstream 2 — Build & Integrate (Weeks 3–8)**
Design and implementation of a conversational intake assistant embedded in your website booking flow. The assistant will handle new-client intake questions, surface relevant service recommendations, and hand off to your team for anything requiring human judgment. Integration with your existing MindBody calendar via their public API.

**Workstream 3 — Handoff & Documentation (Weeks 9–10)**
Staff training session (recorded for future onboarding), written runbook covering day-to-day operation and common troubleshooting, and a 30-day post-launch support window for questions and minor adjustments.
